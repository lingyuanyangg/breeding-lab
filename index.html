How to read

  • Numbers and symbols: T01 ○ red, T02 △ orange, T03 □ yellow, T04 ◇ green, T05 ✕ teal, T06 ＋ blue, matching the HUD badges. ALL 6 means everyone.
  • Pick your number and your cues get a coloured edge, while other players' cues fade and hide their text. Your row lights up in the overview.
  • NEXT preview: 5 s before every event (except observation), the HUD shows NEXT, an empty-ring badge and a 5→1 countdown. The event starts on time, not early.
  • Back to back: at 5:00, 5:20, 7:30 and 8:00 the next cue starts the moment the previous one ends. The HUD shows only a small NEXT line along the bottom edge, so watch for it.
  • Judging: each cue is rated success, partial, fail or rebel, which shapes stability and the final organism. Open “Judging” to see what the system listens for. For noisy sounds, pitch cues use brightness (centroid) instead.
  • Light: the chamber turns red at 5:35 and stays red; 8:00–8:15 power cut with Ableton muted; 8:15–9:20 hard flicker; 9:20 back to cold white.
